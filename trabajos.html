Scripts

here my scripting practices

Movement System

A highly responsive character movement system featuring quick dashes in four directions (forward, backward, left, and right) with cooldown management, alongside a sprint system that has its own cooldown mechanics, while preventing the excessive and repetitive use of abilities.

Shop and Economy System

An interactive shop system featuring item purchasing, currency management, and contextual dialogue interactions. It is designed to handle successful purchases, insufficient-funds scenarios, and player interactions through an organized dialogue system.

Combat System

A combat system that includes various types of attacks, such as light and heavy attacks, as well as a combo system. It also features a precision block (parry) mechanic with a cooldown.

Avanced Combat AI

An advanced combat AI designed to behave like a skilled player. It can evaluate combat situations, dodge, parry, punish openings, bait defensive actions, adapt between offensive and defensive behavior, and make strategic decisions during combat while maintaining fair reaction times.

Death Screen System

A damage response system featuring a red vignette that visually warns the player of danger, accompanied by a custom death screen that incorporates game interface transitions.
